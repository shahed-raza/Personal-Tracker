# Personal Tracker

## BASIC Features

- basic logging
- daily & weekly reports
- daily & weekly reminders for re-considering, tracking, reflecting, analysing

## OVERWRAP Features

- roadmaps crud
- clear timelines for roadmaps
- clear steps crud for roadmaps

## what am i creating this for?

### progress tracking

0. Tracker will fall apart, need to assume this
    - just like the author of the book "Learn C The Hard Way" said
    - "He assumes the code he writes is vulnerable, will break and will be exploited"
    - Be sure & ready for not meeting your own expectations (i do this)
    - Be ready to restart again-and-again
    - Keep in mind things will not go as you planned
    - And everything on your checklist won't be done 100%
    - Don't aim for perfection and stop (i do this)
    - Aim for progress, ugly inefficient and minute progress
1. logging whatidid
    - for day-to-day tracking (foundational)
2. important notes
    - so that i don't forget how my state of mind was
    - to prevent keep forgetting like everytime
3. daily tasks clarity
    - to remove the procrastination due to lack of clariy
    - procrastination due to things seeming huge
    - breaking them down into daily achievable tasks and removing mental load
4. daily, weekly, monthly reports on what i did, how's it going
    - quite important as this is what i don't track and know
    - need to know where i'm heading towards
    - important for re-calibration and current state
5. weekly, monthly reflection reminders
    - the reports above give the data (how & what actually happened)
    - this here is for reflection, re-calibrating
    - improving my-self, giving self-talk improving my mental-health condition
6. distractions i face daily
    - a distraction tracker is super important
    - i'd be able to stare at what am i doing wrong
    - what is happening with me
    - why am i facing it
    - a opportunity to clear know yourself
7. problems i face daily
    - to solve a problem i need to understand it
    - so start by clearly stating what it is and accepting the your problem
8. timeline clarity with a calendar
    - i really don't have sense of time
    - to really get a sense of the time
9. roadmaps & timeline & breakdown into milestones
    - to breakdown huge roadmaps into doable chunks, milestones
